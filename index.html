Our story

Three generations, one gate.

Whitegate Dairy Co. started as a six-cow homestead run by Chinna Reddy's grandfather. Today the herd has grown, but the gate at the entrance is still the same one — repainted, never replaced.

We sell what we make ourselves. No aggregating milk from other farms, no repackaging someone else's produce. If a product carries our name, one of our own animals is the reason it exists.
